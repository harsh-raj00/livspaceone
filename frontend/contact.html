Common Questions

How do I book a professional through LivspaceOne?

Simply visit our Services page, browse through our verified professionals, and click "Book Now" on any expert's profile. You can also use our AI Concierge chatbot for instant recommendations based on your project needs.

What areas do you currently serve?

We currently serve 50+ cities across Bihar and neighboring states, with active expansion into Tier-1 metros. Our materials marketplace delivers nationwide across India.

Are the professionals on your platform verified?

Yes. Every professional undergoes a multi-step verification process including identity checks, skill assessments, and portfolio reviews. Verified experts are marked with a blue badge on their profiles.

Do you offer interior design consultations?

Absolutely! We offer free initial consultations with our certified interior design partners. For full-scope projects, our designers will create 3D renderings, mood boards, and complete project timelines for your approval.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI, all major credit/debit cards, net banking, and EMI options. For large projects, we also offer milestone-based payment plans to ensure transparency and convenience.
